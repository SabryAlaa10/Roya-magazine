Editor's note

We would rather publish five pieces worth finishing than twenty worth skimming.

Roya began with a simple observation: students enter the faculty not knowing where to start, what to study, or what the industry expects. Older students have already found those answers the hard way.

This magazine is the next step. It records what we learn, from the news that matters to the experiences of students one batch ahead, so knowledge keeps passing from one batch to the next.
